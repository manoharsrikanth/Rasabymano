M.

Manohar B. Srikanth

Engineer · Inventor · Photographer

A professional practice in technology.
A lifelong attention to how things look, feel, and work.

Manohar Srikanth’s background spans electrical engineering, computer graphics, robotics, and computational photography, with a master’s degree from the Indian Institute of Science and a Ph.D. from MIT.

His professional work includes camera-system architecture at Apple and experience at Meta and Nokia. His public portfolio describes work on cameras behind displays, mixed-reality imaging, and robotic lighting. His photographic practice includes solo exhibitions in Boston.

RĀSA Design Studio is a personal learning initiative: a place to share methods of observation, experimentation, and making. The aim is to help learners develop ideas of their own through attentive guidance and hands-on investigation.

What experience becomes here

A way of thinking together.

01

Ask before prescribing.

Help a learner clarify what they are trying to discover before choosing a tool or a solution.

02

Make the reasoning visible.

Discuss why a material, sensor, dimension, or approach was chosen—and what evidence could change that choice.

03

Treat revision as progress.

A first attempt is something to learn from. Testing and improving are part of the work.

04

Give the work care.

Make room for thoughtful presentation, clear explanation, and pride in something personally understood.

“What did you notice?
What would you try next?”
