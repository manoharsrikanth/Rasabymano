01 / Print & presentation

Ideas, printed at a scale worth discussing.

Print a room plan at a chosen scale, annotate it together, then revise the design.

A3 + SRA3 media · Up to 1200 × 2400 dpi

02 / Laser fabrication

xTool S1 · 40W + 2W IR

From a digital line to a physical assembly.

An enclosed laser platform with a 40W diode module for cutting and engraving suitable materials, plus a 2W infrared module extending engraving to metals. An air filter is part of the studio setup.

A possible investigation

Develop a slotted model or mechanism, test the fit, and discover how material thickness affects assembly.

40W diode · 1064 nm IR engraving

03 / Additive prototyping

Bambu Lab A1 Mini + AMS

Design it. Hold it. Improve it.

Automatic calibration and multicolor filament handling support iterative 3D printing in a compact setup. The studio also holds a substantial supply of printing materials.

A possible investigation

Design a sensor housing or robot bracket, measure the first print, and refine the fit.

CAD-to-object workflow · Automated calibration

04 / Cutting & paper engineering

Cricut Maker 4

Flat patterns. Dimensional possibilities.

A versatile cutting platform compatible with 300+ materials and 13 tools. Available processes depend on the installed blade or accessory; the studio’s exact accessory set is being cataloged.

A possible investigation

Explore folded structures, packaging, stencils, and the geometry of turning a sheet into a form.

Cut · Draw · Score with compatible tools

Look closer. Measure better.

The investigation bench.

Professional digital calipers

Mitutoyo and other makes for dimensions, fit, and tolerance studies. Exact models to be cataloged.

Analog & digital oscilloscopes

Two complementary ways to see changing electrical signals, timing, and circuit behavior.

Electronics & Arduino

Soldering station, multimeters, boards, LEDs, resistors, and supporting components for guided physical-computing projects.

Imaging & optics

USB computer-vision cameras with interchangeable lenses, a digital microscope, and photographic resources drawn from Mano’s separate collection.

Spatial & thermal measurement

Laser distance meters, multi-plane laser level, and digital infrared temperature sensors.

Computing & robotics resources

Mac minis, iMacs, MacBooks, and 300+ outdoor-stored large construction blocks for reconfigurable obstacle courses.

Supporting the experience

Sound, drawing, and sharing.

An A4 pen plotter, HP scanner, Arturia 49-key controller, Alesis Nitro electronic drums, Sony projector and roll-up screen, television, Apple TV, and speaker system extend the studio’s possibilities.

Tools are resources for guided projects, not a promise of independent machine access. Activities and materials are selected for student readiness; laser fabrication and soldering require appropriate supervision. Product images show representative configurations. See image credits and sources.
