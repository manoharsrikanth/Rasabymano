03 / A note from the maker
by
Mano.

Made over time.
Given life by people.

I’ve developed these homes over time, working on the spaces, gardens, materials, and everyday comforts. Each improvement builds on what I learn from the people who stay.

Thousands of visitors have brought life to these places. Some return every year; others several times a year. Welcoming familiar faces back has become one of the pleasures of hosting.

RĀSA brings together my interests in spaces, nature, food, photography, and making. These homes are one expression of that curiosity. This imagined edition is another: a place to explore what could come next.

A little care changes the everyday.

Stay a little
closer to life.
