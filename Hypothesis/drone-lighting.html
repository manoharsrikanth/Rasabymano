The photographic problem

A line of light.
A moving subject.

Rim illumination defines the edge of a subject: a shoulder, a face, the outline of a body. Its effect depends on the relationship between the subject, the camera and the light.

At MIT, I worked on computational rim illumination using aerial robots. Instead of a fixed studio light, an autonomous flying robot could reposition the illumination around the subject.

The robot belongs in the frame because it belongs to the photograph’s creation.
Robotics in service of an image

Judgment becomes
a control problem.

The research connects image analysis, aerial control and photographic intent. Positioning a light becomes an active relationship with the image being made.

This work makes the connection between technology and photography concrete. The engineer and the photographer are working on the same outcome: a particular quality of light.
