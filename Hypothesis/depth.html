04 / Computational imaging

Depth is information.
Experience is the purpose.

Stereo, defocus, light fields and the work of making a computational image useful.

More than a second camera.

In the Nokia multi-camera work, a high-quality primary camera is combined with auxiliary cameras that contribute depth. The architecture separates the job of making the photograph from the job of understanding the scene.

The research paper Depth from semi-calibrated stereo and defocus combines complementary depth cues. The public record also includes multi-camera calibration and defocus effects.

Making depth trustworthy.

At Pelican Imaging, my work included depth regularization and interactive matting using RGB-D images. Noisy depth is useful only when the system can turn it into stable decisions about the scene.

Depth-assisted perspective correction is another example: scene geometry becomes a means of improving the resulting image.
