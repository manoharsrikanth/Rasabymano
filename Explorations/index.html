01 / Art & Inquiry

Give a question
something to become.

Intelligence. Perception. Agency.
Technology as a medium for asking.

By invitation

What changes when
a machine interprets us?

A personal studio for philosophical and artistic exploration, where ideas can move through conversation, sketching, simulation, and making. AI and physical tools give a question a form that can be encountered and challenged.

The outcome might be an installation, an image, a prototype, a written reflection—or a better question.

Possible lines of inquiry

01

Assistance & agency

An object offers help. When does its attention feel welcome, and when does it become a demand?

02

Perception & ambiguity

A camera forms an interpretation. What does it miss, and how can a person question what it concludes?

03

Presence & connection

Light, sound, movement, language. How little does an object need to do before we feel a relationship?

Proposed explorations, not claims of completed work or published research.

The studio behind the inquiry ＋

Thinking & making

Shared table, whiteboard, projection, Macs, coding and AI-assisted exploration.

Physical form

Bambu Lab 3D printing, xTool laser fabrication, Cricut, pen plotting, and Xerox A3 color printing.

Signals & perception

Electronics and Arduino, oscilloscopes, measurement instruments, cameras, lenses, and microscopy.

Image & sound

Photographic resources, lighting, displays, speakers, keyboard controller, and electronic drums.

A personally equipped working space. Specific tools support particular questions; the room can change with the experiment.

From thinking to inhabiting

02 / Landscape & Cultural Memory

What does
an object remember?

Gardens, sculptures, and cultural pieces.
A continuing conversation with place.

By invitation

Heritage, in the present tense.

Asian and South Asian cultural pieces sit within a personal exploration of landscape, sculpture, material, and memory. Their placement invites a slower encounter: with changing light, living plants, and the space around an object.

How can something carry a history
and belong to the life around it now?

A garden can give a piece room to be noticed. A piece can give a garden a point of reflection. The interest lies in that relationship—and in the meanings people bring to it.

What this exploration holds ＋

Landscape and planting; Asian and South Asian sculptures and cultural objects; materials, placement, and changing light. Collection-specific photographs and stories can be added as they are documented.

03 / Culinary & Table

How does a meal
carry a memory?

Ingredients, technique, tools, and company.
The everyday, given attention.

By invitation

From an ingredient
to a shared experience.

Cooking is a place where memory and experimentation meet. Heat, texture, timing, and aroma make abstract decisions immediately tangible.

South Asian references and everyday rituals can sit alongside contemporary techniques. The interest extends from what happens at the worktop to how a meal is served and enjoyed.

What remains familiar?
What becomes possible
when the method changes?

A coffee ritual. A different application of heat. A familiar ingredient, prepared another way. Small experiments offer a way to keep tradition in active conversation with the present.

The culinary tools ＋
  • Rational iCombi Pro XS station with AirVent, water filtration, and dedicated stainless worktop.
  • TurboChef Fire artisanal oven for high-heat baking.
  • BlueStar rangetop with six burners and a grill.
  • Supporting preparation, warming, coffee, and cooking equipment, documented in the separate culinary catalog.
