01 / From zero to one

A different
starting point.

Instead of finding another place for the camera around the display, I conceived an architecture that placed it behind the display. The optical stack and the computational restoration had to be designed together.

My contribution

The concept.
The foundational architecture.
The conviction to begin.

This is the work of originating a possibility, establishing the system that makes it achievable, and carrying it to the point where engineering can realize it.
