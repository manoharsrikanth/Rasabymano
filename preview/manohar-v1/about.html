The architect
and the photographer.

I’m a System & Experience Architect at Apple, with a PhD from MIT in mechanical engineering and a background in computational cameras and robotics.

I’m also a photographer, with solo and group exhibitions at MIT, where I taught photography. These practices meet in light, imaging, and human perception.

Living Spaces is a separate exploration of how places feel and how everyday details shape an experience.
